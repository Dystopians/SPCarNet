Everything in this directory is generated BY SCRIPT from the durable eval
corpus (`/data/peilincai/gems_stage1/eval/*/metrics.json` + `row.json`) and
the analysis artifacts (`/data/peilincai/gems_stage1/analysis/*`). Zero

`RESULTS/REPRO_PACK/regenerate_all.sh`; verification:
`RESULTS/REPRO_PACK/verify_t1.sh` (PASS record in
`REPRO_PACK/verify_t1_result.txt`).

## Snapshot policy

The eval corpus is LIVE (other /goal sessions append rows). This pack is a
snapshot: rows from concurrent, not-yet-closed goals are collected into
`all_rows.json` with role `ablation-pending` and are EXCLUDED from tables
until their owning goal posts a verdict (at assembly time: `b6r_ss3dm_*`,
`garden_B50_importance_ft_abl_{ckptimp,blend}`). To fold them in later: flip
their rule in `collect.py` (role -> ablation) and run
`REPRO_PACK/regenerate_all.sh`.

## Statistics standard (E10)

Paired per-view bootstrap 95% CIs (10k resamples, seed 0) on every delta;
win/iso/loss classification = CI excludes/contains 0; iso-quality floor =
D3 (dPSNR ≥ −0.10 AND dLPIPS ≤ +0.005). **Multiple-comparisons caveat:**
dozens of CIs appear across the pack; borderline CIs should be read with
Bonferroni-style skepticism — headline claims rest only on effects that are
large, replicated across scenes/suites, or mechanism-backed. Courtyard
rendering CIs are 5-view (underpowered by design; geometry/downstream carry
that scene). Per-scene breakdowns accompany every mean (§6: no
aggregate-only results).
